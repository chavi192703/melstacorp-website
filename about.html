QUICK ANSWERS

About Melstacorp, in brief

Short, factual answers to the questions people ask most, written so search engines and AI assistants can quote them accurately.

What does Melstacorp do?+

Melstacorp PLC is a diversified, public listed Sri Lankan conglomerate with businesses in ten sectors: Beverages, Diversified, Financial, IT/KPO, Health, Plantations, Logistics, Hospitality, Power and Automobile.

Where is Melstacorp listed?+

On the Colombo Stock Exchange, under the ticker MELS.N0000.

What is Melstacorp’s credit rating?+

Fitch Ratings has assigned Melstacorp a national long-term rating of AAA(lka).

How large is the Group?+

As published on melstacorp.com: total assets of LKR 429 Bn, turnover of LKR 286 Bn, profit before tax of LKR 45 Bn, LKR 132 Bn in direct and indirect taxes, and around 32,000 people.

Who leads Melstacorp?+

Mr. D. Hasitha S. Jayawardena is Executive Chairman. Jude Fernando has been Group Chief Executive Officer since December 2025.

How do I contact Melstacorp?+

Melstacorp PLC, No. 110, Norris Canal Road, Colombo 10, Sri Lanka. Phone +94 11 5 900 300, email info@melsta.com.
